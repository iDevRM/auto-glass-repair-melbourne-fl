Windshield Repair

A rock chip or short crack in your windshield rarely fixes itself, and Melbourne’s daily temperature swings between air-conditioned mornings and blazing afternoons push small chips into long cracks faster than most drivers expect. Windshield repair uses an injected resin to stop the damage from spreading and restore most of the glass’s original strength.

This is the right call for anyone with a chip smaller than a quarter, away from the windshield’s edge. Ignore it and you are likely looking at a full replacement within a few weeks, especially once summer storms start hitting the glass with rain and grit at highway speed.

Windshield Replacement

When a crack is too long to repair, reaches the edge of the glass, or sits directly in your line of sight, the windshield needs to come out entirely. Replacement means removing the damaged glass and bonding in a new piece with automotive-grade urethane adhesive rated for Florida’s heat and humidity.

Drivers who put this off risk a windshield that can pop out of its frame in a crash, since a cracked windshield loses much of the structural support it’s designed to give the roof. It also means a longer wait for calibration once the damage finally forces the issue.

ADAS Calibration

Most vehicles built since roughly 2018 have a forward-facing camera mounted near the rearview mirror that powers lane departure warnings, automatic emergency braking, and adaptive cruise control. Any time the windshield is replaced, that camera needs to be recalibrated so it reads the road correctly.

Drivers who skip calibration often do not notice a problem right away, but the safety systems can misjudge distance or fail to trigger when they should, which defeats the purpose of having them at all. We calibrate on site as part of the same visit for most common Melbourne-area makes and models.

Side & Rear Window Replacement

Door glass and rear windows break differently than windshields, usually from a break-in, a slammed door with a hidden crack, or a regulator that finally gives out after years of use. This service covers replacing the tempered glass itself and, when needed, the track or regulator that raises and lowers it.

A window that will not seal fully lets rain into the door panel and carpet, which in Melbourne’s humidity can turn into mold inside the door within days. A broken side window is also an open invitation for theft, so this is not a repair worth putting off.
